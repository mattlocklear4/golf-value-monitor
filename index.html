Round & scoring model

Rounds 1–3 use total_points (column L); Round 4 uses scoring_points (column I). Each upload replaces the selected round's DataGolf projections. You must update your own score-to-par prediction separately. No fixed player count.

Tournament scoring standard deviations

All Round Score probabilities use a fixed normal-distribution standard deviation of 2.85 strokes for every player and round. Legacy player-specific standard deviations cannot override it.

The Best screen chooses the highest-ROI Over and Under separately for each player. Different lines are priced independently. Odds are manual snapshots, not live feeds; the last import time for each sportsbook appears above.

Model: strokes = par + your field prediction − (player fantasy points − uploaded field fantasy average) / 4.1 (fixed divisor). Fair odds use a normal approximation. Every player and round uses a fixed scoring standard deviation of 2.85 strokes. These are full-round, pre-round model probabilities; they do not account for holes already completed.
